Cron Expression: Every Hour (0 * * * *)

Execute scheduled tasks precisely at the beginning of every hour (minute 0), 24 times a day.

0 * * * *

Syntax Field Breakdown

FieldValueMeaning
Minute0Exactly at minute 00
Hour*Every hour (0-23)
Day of Month*Every day of the month
Month*Every month (1-12)
Day of Week*Every day of the week (0-6)

Code Snippets

Linux / Unix Crontab (crontab -e):

0 * * * * /usr/bin/python3 /opt/scripts/backup.py >> /var/log/backup.log 2>&1

Node.js (node-cron):

cron.schedule('0 * * * *', () => { console.log('Hourly job executed'); });
